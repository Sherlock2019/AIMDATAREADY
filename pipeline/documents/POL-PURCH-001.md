## 1. Scope
This policy applies to all purchases of packaging, raw materials and services by the company.

## 2. Purchase order approval limits
Purchase orders up to 10,000 EUR are approved by the procurement manager. Orders from 10,000 to 100,000 EUR are
approved by the supply-chain director. Orders above 100,000 EUR also need finance approval. AI agents may propose
purchase orders but may never approve or place them.

## 3. Approved suppliers
Only suppliers with an approved, currently valid agreement in SAP Ariba may receive purchase orders.

## 4. Returns and defective material
Returns of defective material follow the individual supplier agreement. Where the agreement is silent, defects must
be reported to the supplier within 14 days of goods receipt.

## 5. Price changes
Price changes take effect only after a signed amendment to the supplier agreement.
